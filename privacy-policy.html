1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Laura Lehrer ("we," "our," or "the operator") processes information through the Laura Lehrer Social Media Publishing Application ("Application"). The Application is a private, owner-operated tool used to manage and publish approved content to the operator’s Facebook Page, Instagram account, and Threads account using authorized Meta APIs.

The Application does not provide public registration, consumer accounts, or a way for outside users to connect their social media accounts.

2. Information We Process

Depending on the connected platform and workflow, the Application may process:

  • Facebook Page names and identifiers; Instagram and Threads usernames and account identifiers.
  • Authentication tokens, permission information, and token-expiration metadata.
  • Approved post text, captions, images, media references, publication timestamps, content IDs, post IDs, and URLs.
  • Internal content-approval records, including relevant Slack or Telegram approver identifiers and timestamps.
  • Operational logs, publication results, and error information.

The Application is not designed to collect other users’ private messages, comments, or personal profiles. Incidental personal information in content or operational records may nevertheless be processed.

3. Purposes of Processing

  • Authenticate and maintain authorized social media connections.
  • Prepare, review, schedule, and publish approved content.
  • Track publication outcomes, prevent duplicate posts, and troubleshoot errors.
  • Maintain application security and respond to privacy or deletion requests.

We do not sell personal information or use information obtained through Meta APIs for targeted advertising.

4. Third-Party Services

The publishing workflow uses or may use Meta (Facebook, Instagram, Threads), Google Drive and Google Sheets, Cloudinary, GitHub, Slack, Telegram, and Anthropic for account connections, content and media management, internal approvals, version control, and drafting assistance. Information sent to these providers is handled under their applicable terms and privacy policies.

5. Storage and Security

Tokens and certain operational records are stored locally in the operator’s working environment. Some publication and approval records may also be stored in project repositories and connected services. Access controls and other reasonable safeguards are used to reduce unauthorized access. No storage or transmission system is completely secure.

6. Retention

We retain information as reasonably necessary for application operations, publication records, troubleshooting, security, and applicable legal obligations. A single automatic deletion period is not currently applied to all data categories. Records are reviewed and deleted manually when appropriate, including following verified deletion requests.

7. Privacy Requests and Deletion

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information associated with the Application, subject to applicable law and relevant exceptions. Email lauralehrer95@gmail.com with the subject “Data Privacy Request – Laura Lehrer Application” and explain your request. We may ask for limited information to verify your identity or locate relevant records.

We aim to complete deletion requests within 30 calendar days after receipt and any necessary verification, subject to applicable legal requirements. Laura Lehrer personally coordinates the response and confirms completion or explains any lawful retention exceptions.

See our Data Deletion Instructions for further details.

8. Disclosure

Information may be disclosed to the service providers described above when necessary to operate the Application, to comply with valid legal requirements, or to protect the Application and connected accounts.

9. International Processing

Information may be processed in countries where the operator or service providers operate, subject to applicable legal requirements.

10. Children’s Privacy

The Application is not directed to children, does not offer public sign-up, and does not knowingly solicit personal information from children.
