The Laura Lehrer Social Media Publishing Application is a private tool for publishing content to the operator’s own Facebook, Instagram, and Threads accounts. It does not provide public sign-up or allow outside users to connect accounts.

1. Submit a Request

If you believe the Application holds personal information about you, email lauralehrer95@gmail.com.

Suggested subject: Data Deletion Request – Laura Lehrer Application

Describe the information you want deleted and, if known, the relevant platform, account, or content reference. Do not send passwords, access tokens, or unnecessary sensitive information.

2. Verification

Laura Lehrer may request limited additional information needed to verify your request and prevent unauthorized deletion.

3. Review and Deletion

After verification, relevant records will be identified and deleted where technically feasible and legally appropriate. Depending on the request, this may include local records and logs, stored credentials and tokens, publication and approval records, relevant GitHub records, and information in connected services that the operator can delete. Where appropriate, the operator may also revoke Meta account authorization.

Deleting local Application records does not automatically remove public social media posts or copies controlled by independent third-party services. Those may require separate actions.

4. Timeframe and Confirmation

We aim to complete verified deletion requests within 30 calendar days after receipt and any necessary verification, subject to applicable legal requirements and exceptions. Laura Lehrer will personally confirm completion by email or explain why any information must be retained.

5. Revoking Platform Access

An authorized account owner can also review and revoke connected application permissions in the relevant Meta account settings. Revoking access does not automatically delete information previously stored by the Application. To request deletion of those records, use the email above.
